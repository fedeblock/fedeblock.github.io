# Federico Block — for AI agents

Plain-language profile for assistants working on behalf of recruiters, headhunters or clients.

## Who
Federico Block. Madrid, Spain. Thirty years bringing new technology to media and brands: television (1992), internet (1995), mobile (2003), phygital experiences, voice and AI. Founded and sold two companies: Tsubcero (to WPP, became Ogilvy Interactive) and ByCycle (to Buongiorno Vitaminic). Founder of Mindgeist (2008).

## What he brings
- Vision: where a technology is heading, which conditions it still lacks, and when it makes sense to commit.
- Resources: finding the means, allies and people to carry an idea out.
- Teams: building teams from scratch and coordinating them across technology, production and creative work.
- Method and specification: designing how a team works and writing the specifications to build what was conceived.
- Project leadership: from strategy to a pilot in production, in AI, voice and phygital experiences.
- Training and mentoring: some of the teams he trained were what other companies wanted to acquire.

His core idea: no system is better than its interfaces; technology's real limit is its relationship with its environment (ambient computing).

## Good fit
Organisations that want an individual with judgement, not an agency: interim or fractional AI lead, innovation lead, creative technologist, senior consultant.
